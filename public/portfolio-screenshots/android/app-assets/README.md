## Asset Specifications

### Icons
- Size: 512x512 px
- Format: PNG (32-bit with alpha)
- Purpose: Google Play Store app icon

### Feature Graphics
- Size: 1024x500 px
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Purpose: Play Store banner at top of listing

### Screenshots
- Size: 1080x1920 px (9:16 portrait)
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Purpose: Play Store app screenshots (minimum 2, maximum 8)

### Promotional Images
- Size: 1080x1080 px (square)
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Purpose: Social media, marketing materials
